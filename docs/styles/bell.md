# STYLE — Bell（オーナー提示の参照 4 枚から起こした質感仕様、2026-09-04）

参照画像はオーナーが Grok Imagine で生成したもの（本 repo には置かない）。文面は JoyAI / Anima へそのまま渡す英語。

```
Rendering: premium anime key-visual finish, soft painterly shading with smooth gradients
  (NOT flat cel), luminous airbrushed skin with subtle pink blush, semi-gloss highlights.
Lines: thin colored line art (brown/mauve, never hard black), outlines dissolve into color,
  almost no outlines inside hair.
Hair: silver-blonde base fading to vivid pink tips, individually drawn strands and feathery
  twin-tail tufts, bright highlight bands along the crown, soft sheen.
Eyes: large glossy violet-magenta eyes with gradient iris, starlight/galaxy reflections,
  multiple specular catchlights including star-shaped ones, long defined lashes.
Light: strong rim light / backlight on hair and shoulders, sparkles, bokeh particles, soft glow.
Materials: white fur trim rendered fluffy with visible fibers; gold trim with metallic specular;
  gold star accents; white fabric with soft folds and slight sheen.
Palette: white, gold, silver-blonde, pink, violet; accents of teal.
Negative: flat cel shading, thick black outlines, dull matte colors, low detail, chibi (unless asked)
```

使い方: ①各パネル指示の末尾に足す ②JoyAI の参照 2〜6 枚目に参照画像を渡し「match the rendering style of images 2-N」と書く（言葉より確実）。
